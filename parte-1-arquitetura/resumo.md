## Design System
- Paleta: terroso + sálvia + off-white + preto profundo
- Fontes: Inter (display) + Newsreader (corpo)
- Layout: max-width 680px, line-height 1.75, fonte 18–20px
- Componentes: CalloutEvidence, CalloutPreparo, CalloutContraindicacoes, RecipeCard, ComparisonTable, TOC sticky
- Dark mode opcional
- Mobile-first, zero pop-ups agressivos
